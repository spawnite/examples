The platforms scene is a climb. Five platforms rise toward the right under a side camera, each 0.9 m higher than the one before, 2 m wide, with a half-metre gap between them. The hero starts on the ground left of the first platform.

A rise of 0.9 m is more than the 0.6 m step the hero walks up and less than the 1.2 m jump, so each platform takes a jump. A and D walk, and Space jumps. On a touch screen, a joystick and a Jump button show instead.

The scene has no round, so it keeps no score and never ends on its own. The scene is `src/scenes/Platforms.tsx`.
