Field is the scene a run plays on: the neon arena, the soldier and the run's screens over it.

## Reached from

The lobby's Play goes to it, and a restart reloads it, so each run starts on a fresh field with the run it was handed.

## What is in it

- The soldier, a rigged model with its guns over the engine's hero, and a camera held at the source's angle.
- The arena of the run's stage: the floor and its grid, four walls, the stage's blocks and its hazard, as the stage table in `src/rules/stages.ts` lays them out.
- The run's rules, the swarm, the elites and the boss as models, the orbs and drops, the shots and telegraphs, and the bloom.
- The HUD, the level-up cards, the pause menu, the field notes, and the death and victory screens.
