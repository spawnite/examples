## Reached from

The game starts on it. Leaving a run, from the pause menu, the death screen or the victory screen, comes back to it, and so does a field that mounts with no run to start, as after a development reload.

- The soldier as the engine's model, idling, dressed in the look picked, with the gun of the weapon picked at its hand.
- A round platform, the grid and the sky in the stage's colours, and the bloom.
- A camera held on the soldier: left of the middle on a desktop, in the upper half on a phone.
- The game's name, the class rolled for the run, the keys, the look, weapon and stage picks, Play and the board.
